A short voyage

Between Water and Sand

Scroll slowly. The world around you follows where your eyes rest.

I. The Storm

Rain arrives sideways. The deck lifts and drops, and salt finds every seam in your coat. Somewhere under the black water, something large moves without hurry.

Lightning sketches the horizon for a blink — then the dark closes again, thicker than before. You grip the rail and wait for the next flash to tell you where the sky ends.

II. The Desert

Heat replaces the cold. The ocean is gone; only dunes remain, soft and endless, breathing under a hard white sun. Your footprints fill themselves in behind you.

A wind carries fine sand across the ridge like smoke. Far ahead, a shimmer pretends to be water — the old mirage, still patient, still lying.
